Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Hevarvet Shop.

How do I place an order?⌄

Click the price button on any product and you will be directed to our Instagram page @hevarvet_shop. Send us a direct message with the product name and we will process your order immediately.

Do you deliver to my area?⌄

We currently deliver across the region. Contact us on Instagram @hevarvet_shop to confirm delivery to your specific location. We are always expanding our delivery zones.

What payment methods do you accept?⌄

We accept cash on delivery, bank transfer, and various digital payment methods. Full details are provided when you message us on Instagram to place your order.

Can I return a product?⌄

Yes! We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you are not happy with any product, contact us on Instagram and we will arrange a return or replacement. Products must be unused and in original packaging.

How long does delivery take?⌄

Standard delivery takes 1-3 business days. Same-day delivery is available in select areas for orders placed before 12pm. Express delivery options are also available - ask us on Instagram.

Are your products authentic and safe?⌄

Absolutely. All products at Hevarvet Shop are sourced from reputable brands and suppliers. We only carry pet-safe, tested products that meet quality standards. Your pet's safety is our top priority.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale pricing?⌄

Yes, we do offer special pricing for bulk orders and wholesale purchases. Please reach out to us directly on Instagram @hevarvet_shop or via the Contact page with your requirements.

How do I follow your new products?⌄

Follow us on Instagram @hevarvet_shop to stay updated on new arrivals, promotions, and exclusive deals. We post new products regularly and run special Instagram-only offers for our followers.

Our Story

Hevarvet Shop started as a small passion project by dedicated pet owners who struggled to find quality supplies locally. Today, we offer over 300 carefully selected products across 7 categories, serving hundreds of happy pet families.

Why Choose Us

We are not just a store — we are a community. Every product is tested, every customer is valued, and every pet matters. Shop with confidence knowing our team is always here to help via Instagram @hevarvet_shop.

Our Commitment
